# Incident Response Plan

**Status:** Planned template — adapt contacts and procedures before operational use.

## Preparation

List required roles, approved communication channels, evidence-handling expectations, and escalation criteria. Keep real contact details out of this public portfolio.

## Response phases

1. **Identify:** Record the report, time, scope, and confidence.
2. **Triage:** Assess impact and prioritize with the responsible team.
3. **Contain:** Select proportionate, authorized actions and document decisions.
4. **Eradicate and recover:** Remove the cause, restore safely, and monitor.
5. **Learn:** Record lessons, owners, and due dates for improvements.
